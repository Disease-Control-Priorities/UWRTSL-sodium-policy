\begin{frame}{Why This Particular Gap Bites}
\phantomsection\label{why-this-particular-gap-bites}
\justifying
\footnotesize

The source split is not a side input --- it is the \textbf{router} that
decides which intervention touches which sodium, so error propagates
straight into lives saved and into the BCR.

\medskip
\begin{itemize}
  \item Each lever acts on \textbf{one} source (FOPL/targets/tax $\to$ packaged; procurement $\to$ institutional; LSSS/media $\to$ discretionary).
  \item A wrong discretionary-vs-packaged split \textbf{re-weights the entire portfolio}, not one cell.
  \item GDP regression is a \textbf{point predictor with floors} (10\% discret. USA, 2\% packaged Ethiopia) --- it carries prediction error we currently ignore.
  \item Shares are \textbf{time-sensitive} and packaged share is \textbf{growing} (Vandevijvere); a stale share biases the 2026--2050 trajectory, not just the base year.
\end{itemize}

\medskip
\begin{block}{}
\footnotesize
So the question "country data vs.\ regression" is really: \textbf{is the decision (which policy mix to fund) sensitive to how wrong the share is?} That is a value-of-information question.
\end{block}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Separating the Types of Uncertainty}
\phantomsection\label{separating-the-types-of-uncertainty}
\justifying
\scriptsize

Decision-model good practice distinguishes four kinds; conflating them
is the usual error \autocite{briggs2012}.

\medskip
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}
\begin{tabular}{>{\bfseries}p{2.3cm} p{4.6cm} p{4.6cm}}
\toprule
\textbf{Type} & \textbf{In our model} & \textbf{Handled by} \\
\midrule
Stochastic / variability & Individual-level noise & N/A --- we run a cohort life-table, not microsim \\
Parameter & Effect sizes, RRs, \textbf{source shares}, elasticities & Probabilistic sensitivity analysis (PSA) over distributions \\
Heterogeneity & Real differences by age $\times$ sex $\times$ country & Stratify --- already in the model, do \textbf{not} average away \\
Structural & Multiplicative combining, Well$\to$Sick$\to$Dead, GDP-predicts-share assumption & Scenario analysis; report as discrete alternatives \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\medskip
\begin{uncertaintyblock}{Implication for the source-of-sodium debate}
\scriptsize
The missing-data problem spans \textbf{two} types: \textbf{parameter} uncertainty (what \emph{is} the share, given the regression's prediction interval) and \textbf{structural} uncertainty (is GDP even the right predictor for condiment-heavy countries). PSA addresses the first; a named scenario / sensitivity case addresses the second. We should not let one stand in for the other.
\end{uncertaintyblock}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Value of Information: Pricing the Data Gap}
\phantomsection\label{value-of-information-pricing-the-data-gap}
\justifying
\footnotesize

VOI asks:
\textbf{what is the expected cost of deciding under current uncertainty},
and how much of that a better measurement would remove
\autocite{wilson2015voi}.

\medskip
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{EVPI} --- expected value of \emph{perfect} information: the loss from possibly choosing the wrong policy mix because shares are uncertain. An \textbf{upper bound} on what any data collection could be worth.
  \item \textbf{EVPPI} --- partial: isolates the value of resolving the \textbf{source-share parameters specifically}, holding effect sizes etc.\ fixed. This is the number that answers Pending \#4.
  \item \textbf{EVSI} --- sample: value of a \emph{realistic} study (e.g.\ a 24-hr urine sub-study, $n$ and cost specified), net of its cost.
\end{itemize}

\medskip
\begin{exampleblock}{Why this is the right tool for RTSL, not academic decoration}
\footnotesize
It converts "should we fund a survey / push for country data" into \textbf{expected lives or \$ at stake}. If EVPPI on source shares is near zero, the GDP regression is \emph{good enough} and we stop arguing --- a defensible reason \textbf{not} to collect data. If it is large, that is a funded, prioritized line item for the reinvestment proposal: VOI doubles as a data-collection call.
\end{exampleblock}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Proposed Approach \& Decisions for Today}
\phantomsection\label{proposed-approach-decisions-for-today}
\justifying
\footnotesize

\textbf{Minimal, reinvestment-aligned implementation:}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Put a \textbf{distribution} on the GDP-regression share (use Bhat/Marklund prediction interval), not a point estimate.
  \item Run \textbf{PSA} over source shares $+$ key effect sizes $\to$ CIs on deaths/DALYs averted and BCR.
  \item Compute \textbf{EVPPI on source shares} for a handful of representative / high-leverage countries (not all 29).
  \item Report GDP-predicts-share as an explicit \textbf{structural scenario} vs.\ country data where it exists.
\end{enumerate}

\medskip
\begin{alertblock}{Decisions needed}
\footnotesize
\begin{itemize}
  \item Does RTSL want EVPPI in the proposal as a \textbf{data-collection justification}, or only PSA intervals on the headline numbers? (Past skepticism noted.)
  \item Which 3--5 countries are the \textbf{leverage cases} (condiment-heavy + escalating packaged share) for partial VOI?
  \item Scope: full PSA + VOI, or PSA-only for Sep, VOI for the paper?
\end{itemize}
\end{alertblock}
\end{frame}
